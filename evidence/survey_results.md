# PrepSync Survey Results

## Survey Methodology
- **Platform:** Google Forms
- **Distribution:** Shared in WhatsApp groups, Telegram channels, and college placement discord servers.
- **Duration:** 5 days
- **Total Responses:** 47 students

## Demographics Breakdown

### Year of Study
- **3rd Year:** 55% (26 students)
- **4th Year:** 30% (14 students)
- **2nd Year:** 10% (5 students)
- **Graduates/Alumni:** 5% (2 students)

### Branch
- **CSE/IT:** 68%
- **ECE/EE:** 21%
- **Mechanical/Civil:** 11%

### College Tier
- **Tier 1 (IIT/NIT/BITS):** 17%
- **Tier 2 (Top State/Private):** 43%
- **Tier 3 (Local/Regional):** 40%

## Key Findings

- **89%** use 3+ platforms simultaneously for prep
- **76%** feel overwhelmed by scattered resources
- **82%** can't track what topics they've actually covered
- **71%** don't know what specific companies ask
- **68%** have experienced 'preparation paralysis' (not knowing where to start)
- **91%** want a single unified platform
- **74%** would pay for a good solution

## Top 5 Pain Points Ranked by Severity
1. **Lack of structured tracking:** Cannot remember which specific concepts they are weak at.
2. **Resource Overload:** Too many sheets and courses, leading to decision fatigue.
3. **Company-Specific Blindspots:** Preparing generic DSA but failing to answer company-specific CS core questions.
4. **Scattered Revision:** Notes are everywhere (Notion, physical books, bookmarks), making revision hard.
5. **No holistic view:** Can solve LeetCode hards but struggle with basic HR or System Design questions.
